The A blocker is an inference: these interfaces control/observe extant sessions and do not provide a documented first-frame admission barrier for arbitrary future child processes and audio sessions. The native adapter therefore never returns READY. The B blocker is separate: shared capture and an energy threshold do not prove user-speech origin, acoustic echo rejection, an active authenticated voice generation, or measured hardware coexistence.
